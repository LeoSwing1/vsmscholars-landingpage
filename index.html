Before you enrol

What everyone asks

How much is the fee?
The fee depends on the course and the batch, and instalments are available. You get the exact fee structure in writing after the demo class — because by then you know exactly what you are buying. The demo class itself is completely free.
How long is the course?
Duration depends on the course and the batch you choose. In the demo class you get a module-wise roadmap and timeline — what finishes in which week, and when the live projects start.
I know nothing. Does it start from the basics?
Yes. It starts from zero. 12th-pass students, graduates and working professionals all go through the same foundation module — computer and platform basics first, then tools, then projects.
Is there a job guarantee?
We do not hand out false guarantees. What you do get: live client projects, a portfolio worth showing, resume and interview preparation, job referrals and freelancing guidance. Top performers get a shot at an internship at VSM Marketing. The job comes from your work and your interview — which is exactly why we focus on the work.
Are classes online or offline?
Classes are offline, in Meerut — Aakansha Tower, 3rd Floor, Mangal Pandey Nagar. The weekend batch is for students travelling from Noida, Ghaziabad or nearby, or anyone busy through the week. Support material is shared in recorded form.
Do I need to bring my own laptop?
There are systems at the institute to practise on. Even so, we recommend bringing your own laptop — practice continues after class, and setting the software up on your own machine is part of the skill. The trainer will guide you on this at the demo.
Do I get a certificate?
Yes, a course completion certificate. But what actually works in an interview is your portfolio — campaigns that ran, designs that shipped, reels you edited. You leave with both.
What happens in the demo class?
A full live class — sit in, listen, and build along. Meet the trainer, see the batch and the campus, take your course roadmap. No fee, no pressure to sign anything. If you like it, you join; if not, you leave after a cup of chai.

Read this before you enrol

What nobody else tells you.

  • There is no job guarantee. There is placement support — portfolio, interview prep and referrals.
  • Attendance matters. This is a practical course; miss a class and your work falls behind.
  • The certificate is one of completion, not a university degree. The weight sits in your portfolio.
  • We recommend your own laptop. There are systems here, but practice at home is non-negotiable.
  • Fees and the refund policy are given in writing at admission — nothing stays verbal.
  • Seats are limited — not to create pressure, but so the trainer can give every student time.
